Specialized services

Inspections, security & mapping, done responsibly

Aerial Roof Inspections

See your roof without the ladder

We capture detailed aerial photos and video of your roof so you, your roofer, or your insurance company can see its condition:

  • Cracked, slipped, or missing tiles
  • Ridge, eave, and seam wear
  • RV and trailer roofs
  • Before-and-after repair records

Overhead UAS is not a licensed roof inspector. Our images are visual documentation only and do not replace an inspection by a licensed professional.

About us

Two pilots with a passion for flight

Established in 2018, Overhead UAS began when two passionate young drone pilots turned their love of flight into a mission: delivering stunning, high-quality aerial photography, videography, mapping, surveillance, and security solutions for every need.

We work with homeowners, real estate agents, businesses, property managers, and real estate companies. Let us help you capture, monitor, and showcase your property or event from a whole new perspective.
